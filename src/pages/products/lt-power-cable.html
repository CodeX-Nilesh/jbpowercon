04Frequently asked questions

What voltage grade do LT power cables cover?

LT (Low Tension) power cables cover voltages up to and including 1.1 kV — suitable for most building, industrial and infrastructure power distribution.

Can LT power cable be laid underground?

Yes. It can be installed underground or run aerially depending on the site. Armoured versions are typically used underground or wherever extra mechanical protection is needed.

What's the difference between armoured and unarmoured LT cable?

Armoured cable has an additional metallic layer (GI wire or strip) for protection against impact and rodents, commonly used underground or outdoors. Unarmoured cable is typically used indoors or run in conduit.

Which standard applies to JB Powercon's LT power cable?

Our LT power cable is manufactured per IS 70G8 (Part-I), as printed in our product documentation.
