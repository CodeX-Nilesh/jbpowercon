04Frequently asked questions

What does ACSR stand for?

ACSR stands for Aluminium Conductor Steel Reinforced — a bare overhead conductor with aluminium strands around a galvanised steel core.

Why is ACSR used for overhead transmission?

Good electrical conductivity, light weight, fair stress handling, excellent mechanical strength and reduced electromagnetic interference make it well suited to overhead transmission and distribution lines.

How is an ACSR conductor built?

A galvanised steel core carries the mechanical load, surrounded by layers of aluminium strands that carry the current — combining tensile strength with good conductivity.

Which standard applies to ACSR conductor?

It is manufactured per IS 398 (Part 2):2025.
