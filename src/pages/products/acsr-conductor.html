04Frequently asked questions

What does ACSR stand for?

Aluminium Conductor Steel Reinforced — a bare overhead conductor with aluminium strands around a galvanised steel core.

Why is ACSR used for overhead transmission?

A high strength-to-weight ratio, good conductivity and resistance to corrosion make it well suited to long spans and river/valley crossings.

How is an ACSR conductor built?

A galvanised steel core carries the mechanical load, surrounded by layers of aluminium strands that carry the current — combining tensile strength with good conductivity.

Which standard applies to ACSR conductor?

IS 3G8 (Part-II), as printed in our product documentation.
