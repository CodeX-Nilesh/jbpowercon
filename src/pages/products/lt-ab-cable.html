04Frequently asked questions

What voltage grade do LT AB cables cover?

LT (Low Tension) Aerial Bunched cables cover voltages up to and including 1100 V, for overhead power distribution.

Where are LT AB cables typically used?

Low-voltage and medium-voltage overhead power distribution lines in villages and remote communities.

How is LT AB cable different from bare conductor?

The conductors are insulated and bunched together, improving safety, reducing unauthorized tapping and power theft, and looking tidier than bare overhead conductor.

Which standard applies to LT AB cable?

It is manufactured per IS 14255:1995.
