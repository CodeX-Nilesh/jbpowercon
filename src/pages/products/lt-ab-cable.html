04Frequently asked questions

What does "aerial bunched" mean?

Aerial Bunched (AB) cable bundles insulated phase conductors around a messenger wire, so they run overhead as a single cable instead of separate bare conductors.

What voltage does LT AB cable support?

Low-voltage applications up to and including 1.1 kV, typically used for residential and commercial overhead distribution.

Why choose AB cable over bare conductor?

Insulated conductors improve safety, reduce the risk of faults and power theft, and look better than bare overhead wires.

Which standard applies to LT AB cable?

IS 14255 — Aerial Bunched Cables, for working voltages up to and including 1.1 kV.

How do I get a quote for LT AB cable?

WhatsApp or call us with your required conductor size, bunching (duplex/triplex/quadruplex) and approximate quantity, or use the enquiry form on our Contact page.
