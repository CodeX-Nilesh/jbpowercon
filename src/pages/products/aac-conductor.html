04Frequently asked questions

What does AAC stand for?

AAC stands for All Aluminium Conductor — a bare overhead conductor made entirely of aluminium strands, with no steel core.

How is AAC different from ACSR?

AAC has no steel core, so it is lighter and more flexible than ACSR but has a lower strength-to-weight ratio — it suits shorter spans where pole or support spacing is close, rather than long transmission spans.

Where is AAC typically used?

Short-span overhead electrical power distribution lines in cities and towns, as well as rural areas and temporary setups.

Which standard applies to AAC conductor?

It is manufactured per IS 398 (Part 1):1996.
