02Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between LT power cable and LT AB cable?

LT power cables are insulated and often armoured cables laid underground or in conduit. LT AB cables carry the same low-tension supply overhead, bunched around a messenger wire instead of run as bare conductors.

What standard applies to ACSR conductors?

ACSR (Aluminium Conductor Steel Reinforced) conductors are manufactured to IS 398 (Part 2).

Can JB Powercon manufacture to a custom specification?

Yes — conductor size, core count and armouring can be built to your drawing or IS specification.
