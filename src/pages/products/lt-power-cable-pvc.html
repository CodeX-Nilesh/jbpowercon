04Frequently asked questions

What voltage grade does this LT power cable cover?

It covers working voltages up to and including 1100 V, suitable for house wiring and general power distribution.

Where is PVC insulated power cable typically used?

Internal house wiring, lighting circuits, ceiling fans and plug sockets, as well as general power distribution, HVAC equipment and lighting in offices, malls and public complexes.

Is PVC insulated cable fire-safe?

Fire-safety variants are available — ask our sales team about the fire safety options for your specification.

Which standard applies to this cable?

It is manufactured per IS 1554 (Part 1):1988.
