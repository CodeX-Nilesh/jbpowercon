What we do

From conductor drawing to the finished reel, tested at every stage.

JB Powercon runs manufacturing and in-house quality testing under one roof at Chamrail, Howrah, so every cable and conductor leaving the works is checked before it ships.

01Capabilities

01

Manufacturing

Wire drawing, stranding, insulation and sheath extrusion, and armouring, carried out in sequence on-site.

02

In-house testing

HV, insulation resistance and tensile testing on our own equipment before dispatch — see our test lab below.

03

Bulk & project supply

Scheduled supply for utility, EPC and industrial projects, from single reels to bulk orders.

04

Custom specifications

Conductor size, core count and armouring built to your drawing or IS specification.

05

Technical support

Our team helps you select the right cable or conductor for the application before you order.

06

Logistics & delivery

Reel packing and dispatch coordinated to your site schedule across West Bengal and beyond.
